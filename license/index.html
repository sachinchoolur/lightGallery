If you have a v1 or v2 license, you need to purchase a new license for version 3. Key Points

Please find the key points of the commercial license below. If you have any more questions, please contact at contact@lightgalleryjs.com

01

Team member

Commercial licenses are priced per team member. A team member is an individual person permitted to make modifications for your applications that uses lightGallery, whether such person is your employee or a consultant or contractor providing services to you.

02

Free updates

You are entitled to receive all updates within the major version you purchased, for example every 3.x release, as well as later versions if we authorize. A v1 or v2 license does not cover version 3: v3 needs a new license. If you bought your license in the last 6 months, email us at contact@lightgalleryjs.com to get one.

03

Usage in saleable products

You need a team or organization license to use lightGallery in a product which end users can be charged for.

04

Number of products

With the Hobby license, lightGallery can be used as part of a single product. With the team license, lightGallery can be used as part of up to 5 products and with the organization license, lightGallery can be used as part of an unlimited number of products.

Note: This use is limited within the same company.

05

Reselling

lightGallery cannot be used in a product offered for sale or for free, where lightGallery contributes to the core value of the product being sold. If you like to build something based on lightGallery such as a pro WordPress plugin for lightGallery, please reach out to us at contact@lightgalleryjs.com

lightGallery may not be used for re-selling, sub-licensing, or sharing purposes, and cannot otherwise be redistributed on its own (even for free).

06

Unlimited time

The commercial License is not limited in time. Once you purchase a license, you can use it for life under the terms of the particular license.

07

No exclusivity

The commercial License is not exclusive and other buyers may purchase lightGallery.

08

Support

Organization and team license holders will get faster response and priority for reported issues and questions
